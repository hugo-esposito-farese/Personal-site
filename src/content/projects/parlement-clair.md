## What it does

You type the name of a French law and within seconds the app generates a clear summary showing what was in place before, what's changing, and an explanation tailored to who you are — whether you're 10 years old, an average citizen, or someone looking for the legal details.

The goal: for any French citizen to understand a law that affects them without needing a lawyer.

## How it works

1. Enter the name of a French law
2. The app retrieves what the legislation looked like before
3. It surfaces what's actually changing
4. You pick your level — child, citizen, or legal — and get an explanation written for you

## Current state

Live as a demo. The core flow works end-to-end. The focus is on validating whether people find the explanations genuinely useful before expanding the law database.
